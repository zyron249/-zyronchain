ZYRONCHAIN · PHONE WALLET — TESTNET

Create and keep your keys on this device.

Keys are generated here with the device's secure random generator and stored only as an encrypted vault. No account, no server, no app store.

  • Testnet only and unaudited. Do not use it for real value until an independent review is done.
  • This app stays offline: it does not show a balance and it does not broadcast. A no-value testnet RPC exists for the Snap and the CLI. You can create, back up, show your address and sign transfers on this device.
  • Same derivation as the ZyronChain MetaMask Snap (m/44'/249249'/0'/0/0): the same recovery phrase gives the same address in both.

Step 2 of 4

Before you reveal your recovery phrase

  • Anyone with these words controls the wallet and everything sent to it.
  • ZyronChain support will never ask for them.
  • Never send them on Telegram, Discord, X, email or to "support".
  • Never enter them on airdrop, validator or "wallet check" sites.
  • Write them on paper, in order, and keep the paper offline.
  • Make sure nobody can see your screen and no camera is pointed at it.

A web app cannot block screenshots or screen recording. Do not take any.
