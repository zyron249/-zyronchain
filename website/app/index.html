Zyron Wallet TESTNET · UNAUDITED

Scam warning Nobody from ZyronChain will ever ask for your recovery phrase or password: not support, not an airdrop, not a "validator". Anyone who asks is stealing. There is no token sale and no public network with real value yet.

Your ZyronChain wallet, on your phone.

Keys are created on this device with its secure random generator and stored only as an encrypted vault. No account, no server, no app store.

  • Testnet only. This code is unaudited: do not use it for real funds until an independent review is done.
  • There is no public RPC yet, so balances and broadcasting are disabled. You can create, back up, receive-address and sign transfers offline.
  • Same derivation as the ZyronChain MetaMask Snap (m/44'/249249'/0'/0/0): the same 12 words give the same address in both.

1 / 3 · Choose a password

The password encrypts the wallet on this phone. It cannot be recovered; your 12 words are the real backup.

12+ characters, 6+ different characters, about 60+ bits (same rule as the CLI).

2 / 3 · Write down your 12 words

Write these words on paper, in order, and keep it offline. Do not screenshot, photograph, copy, e-mail or store them in a cloud note. Anyone with these words owns the wallet. They are shown only once.

    3 / 3 · Prove your backup

    Type the requested words from your paper. The wallet is saved only after this check passes.

    Restore from recovery phrase

    Enter your 12 words (24 also work) separated by spaces. The same words used in the ZyronChain MetaMask Snap give the same address.

    Unlock wallet

    Forgot the password?

    The password cannot be reset. Delete this vault and restore from your 12 words with a new password.

    Your address

    The QR code holds the plain lower-case address, which is what the chain uses. The mixed-case form above is a display checksum (docs/ADDRESS_CHECKSUM.md).

    Balance and sending

    No public RPC yet: balances cannot be shown and nothing can be broadcast from this app. Signed transfers below are files you can submit later with the CLI (tx-submit) once a public network exists.

    Sign a transfer offline

    Produces exactly the L1 transfer format. Chain ID and your next nonce must be entered by hand from an official announcement. Only transfers can be signed; mining claims are impossible.

    Security
    
        
      
          
    • Only an encrypted vault is stored on this phone (scrypt N=2^17 + AES-256-GCM). The key exists in memory only while unlocked.
      • 
          
    • Auto-locks after 5 minutes without activity, or 60 seconds after the app goes to the background.
      • 
          
    • Copied text is cleared from the clipboard after 60 seconds (when the browser allows it).
      • 
          
    • iPhone/iPad: the browser may delete site data if storage runs low or the app is removed. Your paper backup of the 12 words is the only real backup.
      • 
        
    
        
    
        
      
    
    
    

    
    
    
      
    Delete wallet from this phone?
    
      
    This erases the encrypted vault from this device. Without your 12 words written down, the wallet and anything sent to it are lost forever.
