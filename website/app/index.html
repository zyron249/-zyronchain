ZYRONCHAIN · PHONE WALLET — TESTNET

Create and keep your keys on this device.

Keys are generated here with the device's secure random generator and stored only as an encrypted vault. No account, no server, no app store.

  • Testnet only and unaudited. Do not use it for real value until an independent review is done.
  • No public RPC yet: no balances and no broadcasting. You can create, back up, show your address and sign transfers locally.
  • Same derivation as the ZyronChain MetaMask Snap (m/44'/249249'/0'/0/0): the same recovery phrase gives the same address in both.

Step 1 of 4

Choose a device password

Password protects the encrypted vault on this device only. It cannot be reset.

Recovery phrase (next step) restores ownership on any device. It is the real backup.

Strength: enter at least 12 characters.

12+ characters, 6+ different characters, about 60+ bits (same rule as the CLI).

Step 2 of 4

Before you reveal your recovery phrase

  • Anyone with these words controls the wallet and everything sent to it.
  • ZyronChain support will never ask for them.
  • Never send them on Telegram, Discord, X, email or to "support".
  • Never enter them on airdrop, validator or "wallet check" sites.
  • Write them on paper, in order, and keep the paper offline.
  • Make sure nobody can see your screen and no camera is pointed at it.

A web app cannot block screenshots or screen recording. Do not take any.

Step 3 of 4

Write down your recovery phrase

Words are hidden. Tap a card to reveal it, or press and hold "Hold to reveal all". Words hide again after 20 seconds, or when you leave the app.

    Keep pressing to show every word. Words hide when you release.

    Strength: enter at least 12 characters.

    Security

    • Only an encrypted vault is stored on this device (scrypt N=2^17 + AES-256-GCM). The key exists in memory only while unlocked.
    • Auto-locks after 5 minutes without activity, or 60 seconds after the app goes to the background.
    • Only your address and signed transfers can be copied; the clipboard is cleared automatically after 60 seconds when the browser allows it.
    • iPhone/iPad: the browser may delete site data if storage runs low or the app is removed. Your paper recovery phrase is the only real backup.
