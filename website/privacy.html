Privacy

Privacy policy

zyronchain.com is a static documentation site with local tools. It has no accounts and collects no wallet secrets. Hosting and CDN providers still see ordinary request data, described below.

1. What the site and its apps collect

  • No wallet secrets. No page asks for, receives or stores a recovery phrase, private key, wallet password or keystore. Pages cannot make network requests from script (connect-src 'none').
  • No accounts, analytics, advertising or tracking. No cookies are set, and no analytics or telemetry scripts are loaded.
  • Desktop Wallet Setup and Validator Launchpad build scripts in your browser. What you type (labels, genesis, peers) stays in the page and is not sent anywhere.

2. Phone Wallet — Testnet (on your device only)

  • The recovery phrase and keys are created on your device. Only an encrypted vault (scrypt + AES-256-GCM) is stored, in this browser's IndexedDB for zyronchain.com. Nothing is uploaded.
  • No localStorage, sessionStorage or cookies are used. The service worker caches only the app's own files and never reads the vault.
  • Copying an address or a signed transaction uses your clipboard, which the app tries to clear after a short time. Other apps on your device may still read the clipboard while it holds data.
  • The project cannot see, recover or reset your phrase, password or vault. Removing the wallet, clearing site data or uninstalling the app deletes the vault from this device; your phrase is then the only way back.

3. Hosting and CDN logs (infrastructure, not the app)

The site is served by a static hosting provider (Render) behind a CDN (Cloudflare). Like any web server, they process request data such as IP address, user agent, requested URL, time and TLS metadata to deliver pages, cache content and block abuse, and may keep logs under their own policies. The project does not add analytics on top of these logs and does not sell or share them. It cannot promise what those providers retain; see the Render and Cloudflare privacy policies.

4. Third-party sites

Links to GitHub, X and Telegram leave this site. Those services apply their own privacy policies. Pages send no referrer (Referrer-Policy: no-referrer).

5. Contact and changes

Questions go through the public repository: GitHub issues. ZyronChain support will never ask for your recovery phrase, key or password. Changes to this policy are made in the public repository, where their history is visible.
