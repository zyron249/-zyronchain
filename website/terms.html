Public testnet governance authorized does not mean the public network is activated. Authorized does not mean activated. There is no hosted public testnet, public RPC, explorer or faucet, and no mainnet. Mining is retired. Signing a transaction locally does not broadcast it.

The Phone Wallet is testnet software and has not been independently audited. Do not use it for real value. You alone hold your recovery phrase: if you lose it and the device vault, access is lost permanently, and nobody can reset or recover it.
